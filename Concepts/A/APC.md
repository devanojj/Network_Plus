Angled Physical Contact

The ferrule (the tip of the fiber connector) is polished at an 8-degree angle.
That slight angle ensures that any reflected light bounces outward into the fiber's cladding instead of reflecting straight back down the core toward the light source.
APC connectors, cables, and their corresponding ports are almost universally colour coded Green.
