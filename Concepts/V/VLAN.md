*Native VLAN:* VLAN used to handle untagged frames on a truck port
*Voice VLAN:*  QoS for VoIP

*Inter-VLAN routing:* One physical interface connects to a switch trunk port and handles traffic for multiple VLANs via 802.1Q tagging. Each [[Subinterface]] gets its own IP and VLAN ID.

12-bit VLAN ID field, limiting networks to 4,096 unique VLANs
